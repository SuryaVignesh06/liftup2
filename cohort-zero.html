The Objective

Institutional competitive advantage through product thinking

India's engineering institutions are at an inflection point. The skills demanded by top recruiters on Day 1 are no longer about syntax or frameworks. They are about product thinking, systems awareness, and the ability to ship.

Conducted in 2026, Cohort Zero was Orionac's critical proof of a new learning philosophy: Systems Thinking First. By removing the prerequisite of prior coding knowledge, 107 students across five branches deployed live AI search engines in under 100 hours.

"LiftUp does not replace the curriculum. It adds the one thing the curriculum cannot easily provide — the experience of shipping something real, under pressure, as a team."

02

The Hypothesis

Understanding > Coding

If students understand how an API passes data, how a search index ranks results, and how scraping retrieves information — the code becomes secondary. The real multiplier is intuition about systems.

Cohort Zero was designed to prove exactly this. No prerequisite coding knowledge. No syntax bootcamp. Only systems thinking, scaffolded by instructors who guided what the system expects — not what the code looks like.

03

The Architecture

Tools & Methodologies

Participant Mix

  • CSE & IT — Core Computer Science
  • AI & ML — Emerging Specialists
  • CSBS — CS with Business Systems
  • AITS — Applied Specialisations

Tech Stack

  • Google Stitch — Rapid application layering
  • Google Antigravity — Production deployment
  • Custom LLM Chains — For parity processing

Students spent more time debugging their own logic than being lectured. Instructors acted as architects — guiding "what the system expects" rather than providing syntax fixes.

This created the deep intuition needed for high-pressure shipping. Daily standups, sprint-style deadlines, and zero tolerance for "passengers" gave the cohort a startup-grade execution environment.
