The Objective

Conducted in 2026, Cohort Zero was Orionac's critical test of a new learning philosophy: Systems Thinking First. By removing the prerequisite of prior coding knowledge, we successfully enabled 107 students across five engineering branches to deploy active AI search engines in under 100 hours of development.

01

The Opportunity

Institutional competitive advantage

India's engineering institutions are at an inflection point. The skills being demanded on Day 1 are no longer about syntax. They are about product thinking and systems awareness. Institutions that bridge this gap first will lead placement outcomes for the next decade.

"LiftUp does not replace the curriculum. It adds the experience of shipping something real, as a team, under pressure."

ThePlacement Signal

  • Recruiter demand: Focus on "shipped products" vs GPA.
  • Industry Speed: Bridging the gap between curriculum and AI.
  • Brand Credibility: Signalling industry readiness to the market.
02

The Hypothesis

Understanding > Coding

If students understand how an API passes data, how a search index ranks results, and how scraping retrieves information—the code becomes secondary. Cohort Zero proved that systems thinking is the real multiplier.

03

The Architecture

Tools & Methodologies

PARTICIPANT MIX

  • CSE & IT (Core Computer Science)
  • AI & ML (Emerging Specialists)
  • CSBS (CS with Business Systems)

TECH STACK

  • Google Stitch: Rapid Application Layering
  • Google Antigravity: Production Environment

Students spent more time debugging their own logic than being lectured. Instructors acted as architects—guiding "what the system expects" rather than giving syntax fixes. This created the deep intuition needed for high-pressure shipping.
