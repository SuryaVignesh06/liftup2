Overview

LiftUp is a systems-first AI engineering model where students move beyond learning and into real execution.

They work in real environments, build end-to-end systems, and ship production-grade AI products. By focusing on how systems work—not just code—LiftUp turns students into builders. We don’t teach syntax. We teach systems.

The Opportunity

Why institutions that act now will lead placements in 3 years

India's engineering institutions are at an inflection point. The companies that hire their graduates are changing — fast. The skills being demanded on Day 1 are no longer about syntax or frameworks. They are about product thinking, systems awareness, and the ability to ship. Institutions that bridge this gap first will see the placement outcomes that define their reputation for the next decade.

Placements are the #1 priority for institutions.

Every institution's reputation is built on where its students go after graduation. Companies are increasingly filtering for students who have shipped real products — not just those who scored well. LiftUp directly improves that metric.

Industry is moving faster than curriculum can keep up.

AI, product-led development, and startup-style execution are now mainstream in tech companies. Institutions that equip students with these skills become the preferred recruiting ground.

A new credibility signal is emerging.

The institutions that run programs like LiftUp are signalling to the market: our graduates are ready. That signal compounds. It attracts better companies, better students, and stronger institutional brand.

This is not just about coding. It is about output.

LiftUp does not replace the curriculum. It adds the one thing the curriculum cannot easily provide — the experience of shipping something real, under pressure, as a team.

Goal 2026

Objective: Prove the hypothesis — if students understand how systems work, anyone can ship a real product. 107 first-years with zero coding experience built a live AI search engine in 4 days.

1. Selection

107 students hand-selected from CSE, IT, AI&ML, AI&DS, and CSBS branches.

2. Tools

Built entirely on Google Stitch (AI app builder) and Google Antigravity (deployment).

3. Sprint Format

Startup-standard daily standups. Sprint-style deadlines. Every team ships — no exceptions.

4. Systems First

Instructors guided direction — not answers. Students debugged their own mistakes.

5. Product Output

AI-powered search engine modelled on Perplexity. Live data, LLM processing, real-time output.

6. Results

100% of teams deployed a live product. 60% Perplexity parity. MOU signed with institution.

The Builder Network connects top performers from all cohorts with real startups hiring across India.

Teams that consistently ship gain visibility beyond their cohort.

This is the long-term play — a pipeline from campus to company, validated by output, not degrees.
